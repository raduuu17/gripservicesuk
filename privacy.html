Privacy notice

Who we are

Gripservices UK Ltd (“we”), registered in England & Wales, company number 05931221. Contact: gripservices@mac.com.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry through our website we receive the details you enter: your name, email address, phone number (optional), production or company name, shoot dates, location and your message.

Why we use it

Only to reply to your enquiry and, if you book with us, to arrange the work. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries and taking steps at your request before entering into a contract.

Who handles it

Enquiries are passed to us by email through a trusted form-to-email service used only for that purpose. We do not sell your data or use it for marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for up to 12 months, or longer if they lead to a booking and we need them for our records.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you by contacting gripservices@mac.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk).

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies.
