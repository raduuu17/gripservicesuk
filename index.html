About

The team

Gripservices UK Ltd was established in 1995 and is run by key grips Jess Saunders and Bobby Brown. We supply telescopic camera cranes, dollies, track and remote heads, the best car rigging systems in the UK and custom-built rigs for any situation, together with an experienced NVQ Level 3 grip crew, for film, television, commercials, fashion and stills.
